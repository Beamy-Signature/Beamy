# BEAMY Catalogue — Client User Guide

A plain-English guide to the BEAMY website and the private catalogue you use to keep it up to date.

You do **not** need to know how to code. If you can send a WhatsApp message and upload a photo from your phone, you can run this.

---

## 1. What you have

BEAMY has **two separate places**. They look related, but they do different jobs.

| Place | What it is | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The website** | The public shop. Customers browse collections, look at designs, add pieces to a bag, and order on WhatsApp. | Anyone |
| **The catalogue** | Your private back office. You add designs, photos, prices, homepage images, and contact details here. | You (and anyone you give an account) |

There is **no card payment** on the website. Customers finish every order on **WhatsApp**.

### Your links

- **Website:** [https://beamy-eight.vercel.app](https://beamy-eight.vercel.app)
- **Catalogue:** [https://beamy-cms.vercel.app](https://beamy-cms.vercel.app)

Bookmark both. The catalogue will ask you to sign in. The website will not.

If a page on the website looks empty or old after you save something, wait a few seconds and **refresh** the website (pull down on your phone, or press the refresh icon).

---

## 2. How customers use the website

This is what the public sees. You do not need to “publish the website” each time — it updates itself when you save in the catalogue.

### Pages they can visit

- **Home** — large rotating photos, Men and Women, featured designs, a short story about BEAMY, the lookbook grid, and client notes.
- **Collections** — all collections you have made public.
- **Men** and **Women** — the two main homes for your pieces. Customers can tap category names (such as Agbada or Corporate Suits) to narrow the list.
- **A single design** — photos, price, description, **Add to bag**, and **Order on WhatsApp**.
- **About** — the longer BEAMY story (you write this in Website details).
- **How it works** — consultation, measurements, cloth, making, fitting.
- **Contact** — phone, WhatsApp, email, Instagram, and Lagos address (all from Website details).
- **Bag** — the pieces they have chosen, plus a short form, then **Send order on WhatsApp**.

### The bag and WhatsApp

1. The customer opens a design and taps **Add to bag**, or orders that piece straight away on WhatsApp.
2. In the bag they can change quantities or remove items.
3. They fill in name, phone, delivery address, occasion or date, and any notes (measurements, fabric wishes, and so on).
4. They tap **Send order on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with a ready-made message that includes the pieces, the total, and their details.
5. You continue the conversation as you always have — cloth, timing, fittings.

If a piece is **Price on request**, it still goes into the bag. The message will say the price will be agreed with you.

The bag is saved on **that phone or computer**. If they switch devices, the bag will be empty there.

## 3. Signing in to the catalogue

Open the catalogue link. You will see **Sign in**.

1. Enter the email for your catalogue account.
2. Enter your password. Tap the **eye** if you want to see what you are typing.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

### First time — create an account

1. On the sign-in page, tap **Create a catalogue account**.
2. Enter your email and a password of **at least 8 characters**. Type the password twice so they match.
3. Tap **Create account**.
4. Check that email (and spam). Open the confirmation link.
5. You will be asked to **sign in**. After that, the catalogue opens.

### Forgot password

1. On sign in, tap **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and tap **Send reset email**.
3. Open the email and set a new password (again, at least 8 characters).
4. You will be taken into the catalogue once it is saved.

### Sign out

On the left menu (or the phone menu), tap **Log out** when you are finished, especially on a shared computer.

---

## 4. Finding your way around

After you sign in you will see a gold line at the top and a dark menu.

**On a computer:** the menu stays on the left.

**On a phone:** tap the three-line button at the top right to open the menu.

The menu is:

1. **Dashboard** — a snapshot of how many designs you have.
2. **Designs** — every piece. Add, edit, show, hide, or remove.
3. **Collections** — Men, Women, and any extra groups you create.
4. **Categories** — smaller groups *inside* Men or Women (Agbada, Corporate Suits, and so on).
5. **Homepage images** — the large rotating banner at the top of the website.
6. **Lookbook gallery** — the photo grid lower down on the homepage.
7. **Client notes** — kind words from clients, shown on the homepage.
8. **Website details** — phone, WhatsApp, email, Instagram, address, and homepage wording.

**View website** in the menu opens the public site so you can check your work.

When you save, create, or remove something, a **popup** appears on a paper card with a gold line. Read it, then tap **Continue**. If you are about to remove something, you will be asked to confirm first. Tap **Keep this** if you change your mind.

## 5. Dashboard

This is the welcome screen.

You will see numbers such as:

- **Total designs** — everything in the catalogue, including drafts.
- **On the website** — pieces customers can see.
- **Drafts** — pieces you have saved but not shown yet.
- **Collections** — how many collections you have.
- **On the homepage** — pieces you have chosen to feature.

Tap a number to jump to that list. Tap **Add New Design** when you want to add a piece straight away.

## 6. Designs (the heart of the catalogue)

This is where you spend most of your time.

### All Designs

You will see every piece, with its category and price.

You can:

- **Search** by name, then tap **Show**.
- Show **All designs**, only those **On the website**, only **Drafts**, or only those **On the homepage**.
- **Edit** a piece.
- **Show on website** / **Hide from website**.
- **Show on homepage** / **Take off homepage**.
- **Remove** — this is permanent. You will get a confirmation popup.

**On the website** means customers can find it.  
**Draft** means it is saved for you, but hidden from the public.  
**On homepage** means it also appears in the featured row on the home page. A piece should usually be on the website as well if you want people to open it from there.

### Add a new design

1. Go to **Designs**.
2. Tap **Add New Design**.
3. Fill in the form (see below).
4. Tap **Save design**.
5. Wait for the popup that says it was added.

### Edit a design

1. Find it in **All Designs**.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Change what you need.
4. Tap **Save design**.
5. Refresh the public website if you do not see the change yet.

### What each field means

Every box has a short note under the title. In simple terms:

**Design name**  
What shoppers see. Example: *BEAMY Signature Black Suit*.

**Photos**  
Tap the dashed box and choose photos from your phone or computer. You can add more than one. Any photo type is fine (including photos from an iPhone). Each file must be **under 10MB**.

After a photo is added you will see a popup. **You still need to tap Save design** so the photos stay on that piece. If you leave the page without saving, the photos may not stay attached.

To take a photo off a design, tap **Remove** under that picture, then save.

**Description**  
A short story of the piece — cloth, occasion, how it is made.

**Price (Naira)**  
Numbers only, no commas. Leave blank if the price is on request.

**How the price appears**

- **Fixed price** — one amount, for example ₦250,000.
- **Starting from** — “from ₦150,000” when cloth can change the final figure.
- **Price on request** — no number on the website; they will talk to you.

**For**  
Men, Women, or Unisex.

**Collection**  
Where the piece lives. Men and Women are always there. If you skip this, it will sit in Men or Women based on who it is for.

**Show on the website**  
Tick this to make it live. Untick to keep it as a private draft.

**Show on the homepage**  
Tick this to feature it on the home page.

**More options** (tap to open)

- **Category** — optional grouping such as Agbada or Corporate Suits.
- **Additional information** — care notes, lead time, extra detail.
- **Web address** — leave blank and one is created from the name. Only change this if you know you need a different link.
- **Display order** — smaller numbers appear first in the list.
- **Custom WhatsApp message** — leave blank to use the normal order message. Fill this in if this piece should open WhatsApp with special wording.

### Removing a design

Tap **Remove**, read the warning, then confirm. This cannot be undone. Prefer **Hide from website** if you might bring it back later.

## 7. Collections

Collections are the big groups visitors browse — **Men**, **Women**, and any extra story you add (for example *2026 Executive Collection*).

### Men and Women

These two stay in the catalogue on purpose, so the website always has a home for those pieces. You can:

- Rename them (carefully).
- Change the description and photo.
- Hide them from the website if you ever need to.

You **cannot** delete them, and you **cannot** change their web address. That keeps old links working.

### Create a collection

1. Open **Collections**.
2. Under **Create a collection**, enter a name and a short description.
3. Add a photo if you have one.
4. Choose who it is for (Men, Women, unisex, or mixed).
5. Leave **Published** ticked if it should appear on the website.
6. Tap **Create collection**.

### Edit a collection

Change the fields on that collection’s card and tap **Save collection**.

### Hide or remove

Untick **Published** to hide it. To remove an extra collection (not Men or Women), tap **Remove**. The designs inside it are **not** deleted — they simply no longer sit in that group.

---

## 8. Categories

Categories are **smaller filters** inside Men or Women. Examples: Bespoke Suits, Agbada, Kaftans/Senators, Corporate Gowns.

They appear as tap-able names when someone browses Men or Women.

### Add a category

1. Open **Categories**.
2. Enter a name, choose Men or Women, tap **Add category**.

### Edit or remove

Change the name on that card and tap **Save category**.  
**Remove** takes the grouping away. The designs themselves stay in the catalogue; they just will not sit under that name any more.

You do not have to use categories. If a design has no category, it still appears when the customer taps **All**.

---

## 9. Homepage images

These photographs rotate in the large banner at the **top of the website**.

1. Open **Homepage images**.
2. Tap to add photos (same rules: any image, under 10MB).
3. For each photo you can:
   - Write a **caption** (a few words for people who cannot see the image).
   - Tick **Show on website**, or untick to keep it in the list without showing it.
   - **Move up** / **Move down** to change the order.
   - **Remove** (you will be asked to confirm).
4. Tap **Save homepage images**.

Adding a photo is only half the job. **Save the page** or the website will not stay in step.

If there are no homepage images, the top of the site is a dark banner with your headline only — still fine, just without photos.

---

## 10. Lookbook gallery

This is the **photo grid** on the homepage, below “Why BEAMY”.

It works the same way as Homepage images: add photos, caption, show or hide, reorder, remove, then tap **Save gallery**.

Use finished photographs of real pieces when you can. The lookbook is often what people remember.

---

## 11. Client notes

These are short quotes from clients, shown on the homepage.

### Add a note

1. Open **Client notes**.
2. Enter the **client name** and **what they said**.
3. Role and location are optional (for example *Bride*, *Lagos*).
4. A photo is optional.
5. Leave **Published** ticked if it should appear on the website.
6. Tap **Save client note**.

Only **published** notes appear on the website. You can save a note as a draft until the client is happy for it to be public.

To take a note down, untick Published and save, or tap **Remove**.

## 12. Website details

This is how you change phone numbers and homepage words **without touching the public pages one by one**.

Open **Website details** and you can edit:

- **Phone** — shown on Contact.
- **WhatsApp number** — used for every “Chat on WhatsApp” and every order message. Use the number customers already write to, for example `08101657472`.
- **Email**
- **Instagram — fashion** and **Instagram — woman**
- **Address**
- **Homepage headline** — the large line on the home banner.
- **Homepage supporting text** — the shorter line under it.
- **Short about text** — used on the home page.
- **Full about text** — used on the About page.
- **Footer line** — the small line at the bottom of every page.

Tap **Save website details** when you are done. Then refresh the public website.

If WhatsApp orders go to the wrong number, this is the first place to check.

## 13. Photographs — the simple rules

- Use **any photo** from your phone or computer (JPG, PNG, and others, including iPhone photos).
- Keep each file **under 10MB**. If a photo is too large, you will see a calm message asking you to keep it under 10MB. On many phones you can send the photo to yourself and choose a smaller size, or crop it slightly.
- After photos upload, you will see a popup. Then **save the page** (Save design, Save collection, Save homepage images, and so on).
- Clear, well-lit photos of the actual garment work best. Dark or blurry photos make the house look unfinished.
- You can add several photos to one design. The first one is usually the picture on the collection grid.

If a photo does not appear:

1. Check you tapped **Save** after adding it.
2. Refresh the website.
3. Try another photo under 10MB.
4. Sign out and sign in again, then add the photo once more.

## 14. A typical week with BEAMY

**New piece is ready**

1. Catalogue → **Designs** → **Add New Design**.
2. Name, photos, description, price, Men or Women.
3. Tick **Show on the website**. Tick **Show on the homepage** if it should be featured.
4. Save. Open the website, refresh, and check the piece.

**Piece should come off the site for a while**

All Designs → **Hide from website**. It remains in the catalogue as a draft.

**New homepage banner**

Homepage images → add photos → set the order → **Save homepage images**.

**Phone number changed**

Website details → update Phone and WhatsApp number → save.

**A client sent a beautiful message**

Client notes → add their name and the quote → save. Only publish it if they are comfortable with it on the website.

## 15. What you should not worry about

You do **not** need to:

- Edit any code
- Pay for cards or a payment gateway
- Rebuild or “upload” the whole website after each change
- Change Men or Women’s web addresses
- Understand words like server, database, or hosting

If someone asks you to “give them the admin”, you are giving them a **catalogue account** (their own email and password). Anyone with an account can change the live website, so only give access to people you trust.

## 16. If something looks wrong

**I saved, but the website still looks old**  
Refresh the website. Wait a few seconds and refresh again. Check you are looking at the public website, not the catalogue.

**The design is in the catalogue but not on the website**  
Open the design and make sure **Show on the website** is ticked, then save. Also check the collection is published.

**I see a popup that we could not save**  
Try again. If two things have the same name, change the name slightly. Your other work is not lost.

**Sign in says the email or password is not quite right**  
Try Forgot password. Check you confirmed the email if this is a new account.

**The confirmation email never arrived**  
Check spam. Wait a few minutes. Try signing up again only if you are sure the first email was not used.

**WhatsApp opens but the number is wrong**  
Fix **WhatsApp number** under Website details and save.

**A photo failed with a strange message**  
It was probably too large, or the page needed you to sign in again. Keep it under 10MB and try once more after signing in.

**I removed something by mistake**  
Removed designs cannot be brought back from the catalogue. You would need to add the design again, with photos. For that reason, prefer **Hide from website** unless you are sure.

**The page says it is not in the collection**  
The link may be old, or the piece is a draft. Check All Designs.

## 17. Quick checklist before you show someone the website

- [ ] New designs are ticked **Show on the website**
- [ ] Photos are saved on each design (open the design and look)
- [ ] Prices look right (fixed, from, or on request)
- [ ] Homepage images are saved, in the order you want
- [ ] Phone, WhatsApp, email, and Instagram in **Website details** are correct
- [ ] You refreshed the public website on your phone and it looks right

---

## 18. Need a hand

If the catalogue will not open, or a change will not appear after you have saved and refreshed, contact the person who set this up for you and tell them:

- which page you were on (Designs, Collections, and so on)
- what you tapped
- what you expected to see
- what you saw instead (a photo of the popup is helpful)

You do not need to explain any technical terms. “I saved the new suit and it is still not on the website” is enough to start.
